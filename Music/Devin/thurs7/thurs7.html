Thurdays at 7

We did this for about 2-3 months in 2022. Devin would come by and we'd write / arrange / record on Thursdays from 7ish-9:30ish in my shop. Pretty much one song "start to finish" a night. In some cases it was from a blank slate and others an idea was presented or maybe a previous recording reimagined. Mixing was done before I went to bed. The intent was to create a prolific collection of songs ideas to build from, whilst also learning the songwriting, recording and collaboration ropes. However, as successful as it was, it was short lived. I'm sure we'll pick it back up.
